## 摘要
我们研究了以度量星形图建模的分支准一维域中时间相关热方程的透明边界条件（TBCs）。通过将TBCs的经典概念与网络上偏微分方程（PDEs）理论相结合，我们推导出一个精确的顶点条件，以确保交汇处的无阻碍热流。具体而言，我们推导了扩散系数的求和规则，消除了顶点处的热回流。我们使用Crank-Nicolson有限差分方法在数值上验证了该分析模型的有效性，证实了热在网络中平滑、无阻碍的传播。这些结果为低维结构中热扩散的可调控制和优化提供了一个实用的数学框架。特别是，结合TBCs的众所周知的概念和度量图上PDE的理论，我们提出了一个数学模型，为网络中的热扩散提供了控制工具。

## Abstract
We study transparent boundary conditions (TBCs) for the time-dependent heat equation in a branching, quasi-one-dimensional domain modeled as a metric star graph. By combining the classical concept of TBCs with the theory of partial differential equations (PDEs) on networks, we derive an exact vertex condition that ensures unobstructed thermal flow across junctions. Specifically,we derive a sum rule for the diffusion coefficients that eliminates thermal backflow at the vertex.We demonstrate the validity of this analytical model numerically using a Crank-Nicolson finite-difference method, which confirms smooth, unobstructed heat propagation through the network.These results provide a practical mathematical framework for tunable control and optimization of thermal diffusion in low-dimensional structures. In particular, combining the well-known concept of the TBCs and theory for PDE on metric graphs, we propose a mathematical model providing a control tool for thermal diffusion in networks.
