## 摘要
生成式人工智能的快速发展加剧了大型数据中心高效散热的需求。为了控制热流，具有层状结构的热超材料已被广泛使用，这些材料赋予了热导率的各向异性特性。然而，在背景介质中嵌入相邻层之间具有高热导率对比度的系统中，传统的等效介质近似（EMA）往往无法提供准确的预测。在此，我们通过引入两个修正系数推广了EMA，将其有效性扩展到传统EMA先前不适用的情况，即具有背景介质的高对比度热超材料。值得注意的是，我们提出的其中一个系数在数学形式上与光学中的菲涅耳反射系数相同。这使我们能够将热流在其穿透具有高热对比度的相邻层时的“类反射”行为进行解释。我们的研究结果表明，传统上被视为纯粹耗散过程的热扩散，可以通过射线光学的框架直观地理解。

## Abstract
The rapid growth of generative AI has intensified the need for efficient heat dissipation in large-scale data centers. To control heat flow, thermal metamaterials with layered structures have been widely used, which impart the anisotropic properties of thermal conductivities. However, the conventional effective medium approximation (EMA) often fails to provide accurate predictions in systems with a high thermal conductivity contrast between adjacent layers embedded in a background medium. Here, we generalize the EMA by introducing two corrective coefficients that extend its validity to regimes where the conventional EMA was previously inapplicable, i.e., high-contrast thermal metamaterials with the background medium. Notably, one of these coefficients that we proposed has the same mathematical form as the Fresnel reflection coefficient in optics. This allows us to interpret the "reflection-like" behavior of heat flow as it penetrates adjacent layers with high thermal contrast. Our findings suggest that heat diffusion, traditionally viewed as a purely dissipative process, can be understood intuitively through the framework of ray optics.
